# Data

`Demo_comparison_SuiteSparse_full_rank_quantiles.m` downloads the
`ash958`, `well1033`, and `illc1033` Matrix Market archives from the
[SuiteSparse Matrix Collection](https://sparse.tamu.edu/) on first use.

The downloaded files are stored in `data/suitesparse/` and ignored by Git.
The matrices remain subject to the terms supplied by their original authors
and by the SuiteSparse Matrix Collection.
